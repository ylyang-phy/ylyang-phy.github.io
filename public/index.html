Research interest

My research currently focuses on developing techniques to study thermal and dynamical properties of quantum many-body systems, including

  • numerical methods based on tensor networks
  • quantum algorithms, especially for near-term devices.

Please refer to research page for my previous works.


Short bio

I obtained my PhD under the supervision of Prof. J. Ignacio Cirac and Dr. Dr. Mari Carmen Bañuls at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics, in Garching near Munich. Before that, I attended the Master program Theoretical and Mathematical Physics in Ludwig Maximilian University and Technical University of Munich, and bachelor courses in University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in Beijing. I was also a visiting student at St Edmund Hall, University of Oxford.
